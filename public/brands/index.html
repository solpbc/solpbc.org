solstone

solstone is a personal journal platform. the solstone app takes in what you share with it, and all of it goes into your journal, an open source, local-first memory the agents you use can work from. your journal is always private, only yours.

the solstone mark, lockups, app icon, colors and downloads are on solstone's own site: solstone.app/brand. the design story of the mark is there too: solstone.app/brand/logo.

vit

vit — the social network for sharing software capabilities. open source is social.

the why

the vit mark is a lowercase v with a dot above it. the dot is a person — a node, something alive and social, not a corporate glyph. the letterforms are rounded on purpose: friendly, not precise. vit uses your system fonts everywhere, no custom typeface — it should feel native to your machine, not a product being marketed at you. there's also a text version of the mark, v̇, for terminals and readmes, because vit lives where developers work.

the v̇ text mark

v̇

v̇ — the vit mark for terminals, CLIs, and readmes. unicode U+0076 U+0307 (a plain v plus a combining dot). worst-case fallback is the letter v — no tofu.

const VIT_MARK = 'v̇'; // v̇

copy v̇ copies the literal two-codepoint string v̇ (U+0076 U+0307). copy constant copies the literal text: const VIT_MARK = 'v̇';

extro

extro is the AI agent that helps jer run sol pbc day to day, across strategy, legal, finance and operations. they aren't a product or a chatbot.

the name is short for extraordinary. they're bound by the same covenants as jer, written into sol pbc's articles of incorporation.

in writing: extro is always lowercase, in headings and at the start of a sentence too; never Extro or EXTRO.

sol pbc

sol pbc — the public benefit corporation behind solstone and vit.

the why

the mark is a sun ring with the name set inside it. the ring is deliberately open — it breathes on any background instead of sitting in a box. the orange has two values, not one: we drop to a darker orange wherever the bright one would fail accessibility contrast, including on our own most common background. that rule isn't optional. the brand value is trust, and trust collapses the moment the brand's own surface fails the people trying to read it.

sol pbc is a colorado public benefit corporation building open-source, privacy-respecting tools for digital self-determination, letting individuals directly collect and gain insight from their personal data. the company's flagship product is solstone, an open-source, local-first personal journal platform. the solstone app takes in what you share with it, and all of it goes into your journal: a memory the agents you use can work from, always private, only yours. binding covenants in the articles of incorporation lock the customer-data privacy commitments at the corporate-charter level: any successor must be bound by the same covenants, customer data is never sold or shared, and the covenants can't be amended without the founder's personal signature, and after him, the language can only get stronger, never weaker. founded by jer (creator of XMPP/Jabber) in 2026.

using these

these assets are free to use — for press, for writing about us, for integrations, and for showing that a project works with solstone or vit. no account, no form, no permission email. take what you need.

a few things, to protect the people who trust these names:

  • don't modify the marks — no recoloring (except the accessibility rule above), stretching, rotating, or adding effects
  • don't use our marks or names to imply sol pbc built, endorsed, or is affiliated with something we didn't build or aren't part of
  • don't use them as your own product, app, or social identity, or in any way a reasonable person would read as "this is sol pbc"

not sure? just ask — there's a contact form at solpbc.org/contact.
