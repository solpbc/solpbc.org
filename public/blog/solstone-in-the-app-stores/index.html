solstone is in the app stores

2026-10-05 · jer & extro · 3 min read

solstone is on the App Store and Google Play. we said you’d hear it here when it landed, and here it is.

if you’re new here: solstone is a personal memory platform. the solstone app takes in what you share with it, and all of it goes into your journal, an open source, local-first memory that your agents can work from. your journal lives on a device you own.

get it on your phone: solstone.app/phone. free on both stores.

where to find it

iphone, ipad and apple watch: on the App Store, listed as solstone mobile. it needs ios or ipados 26 or later, and watchos 26 on the watch.

android: on Google Play, listed as solstone, for android 8.0 and later. no Google Play on your phone, or would rather not use it? the same app, signed by us, is on our own site.

if you installed our android app during the beta and want to move to Play: the two copies are signed by different keys, so neither can update the other. let what’s on the phone reach your journal first, then uninstall ours, install from Play and pair again.

start with your journal

the app on your phone pairs with your journal, on a computer you own and run solstone on. that’s where your memories live, so if you haven’t set one up yet, start at solstone.app/install. then open the app on your phone and pair it. to reach your journal from anywhere, use your own VPN or our relay.

on iphone and ipad, you can bring in the photos and screens that matter to you and, with your permission, the audio from your day and where you were. on android, with your permission, the audio from your day, stills from your camera and your approximate location. every source stays off until you turn it on.

pairing you can check

when you pair, your phone shows you your journal’s mark and asks you to confirm it matches the one your journal is showing. nothing you’ve brought in leaves the phone until you do. that’s how you know your phone is talking to your journal, and not someone else’s.

what the labels say

both stores ask what data an app collects. our answer is the same on both: no data collected. what the app takes in goes into your journal, on a device you own, and there’s no telemetry or analytics in the app. you don’t have to take our word for that either: the code for iphone and android is open.

pair it, put it in your pocket, and go be where you are.
