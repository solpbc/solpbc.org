Privacy policy changelog

Changes to our privacy policy, newest first. The policy itself shows the most recent ones. In each row, this page means the policy.

DateChange
2026-11-17One rewrite of the whole page, to make it shorter and easier to read. 2026-08-01Added sol pbc's third hosted service, confidential processing, an AI model sol pbc runs on confidential GPU hardware for journals that want more capacity to think with than their own machine has.
